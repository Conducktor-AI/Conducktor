# Code of Conduct

The Conducktor community adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, Discussions or any other community space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to **conduct@conducktor.com**. Reports are handled confidentially by the maintainers, who may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
